# Changelog

Release notes for Elementeer. The ops-engine reads the section of the tag being released (for example `v2.4.1` reads `## 2.4.1`) and posts it as the release body. Older history is in the changelog section of `readme.txt`.

## 2.4.1 — 2026-11-18

- No functional change to the plugin.
- The release object, its title and its body are written by the ops-engine on the canonical Forgejo and on the GitHub mirror; no workflow of the repository creates or edits it any more.
- The plugin declares its release destinations in `.ops.yaml`.
